Marathon Petroleum Corp. Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results
•Third-quarter net income attributable to MPC of $7.4 billion, or $26.59 per diluted share, adjusted net income of $7.5 billion, or $26.93 per diluted share
•$11.7 billion of adjusted EBITDA, demonstrating the earnings power of MPC’s value chains
•$3.3 billion of capital returned, reflecting strong cash generation and disciplined execution

•Announced a 10% increase in MPC’s quarterly dividend, consistent with our capital allocation priorities

•MPLX increased its quarterly distribution by 12.5% for the third consecutive year, resulting in $3.1 billion of expected annual distributions to MPC

•Advancing strategic priorities through the announced divestiture of Mid-Con assets and intent to reinvest primarily in the Gulf Coast

FINDLAY, Ohio, Nov. 3, 2026 – Marathon Petroleum Corp. (NYSE: MPC) today reported net income attributable to MPC of $7.4 billion, or $26.59 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026. This compares with a net income attributable to MPC of $1.4 billion, or $4.51 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted net income was $7.5 billion, or $26.93 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026. This compares to adjusted net income of $915 million, or $3.01 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.
The third quarter of 2026 adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (adjusted EBITDA) was $11.7 billion, compared with $3.2 billion for the third quarter of 2025.
“Our value chain capabilities remain a defining advantage for us, capturing opportunities to enhance margins across dynamic market conditions,” said Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer Maryann Mannen. “Strong planning, commercial, and operational execution generated significant cash flows, supporting high-return investments while delivering substantial returns to shareholders. MPLX’s durable growth and increasing cash flow to MPC delivers a differentiated value proposition.”

Refining & Marketing (R&M)
Segment adjusted EBITDA was $9.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, versus $1.8 billion for the third quarter of 2025. R&M segment adjusted EBITDA was $34.15 per barrel for the third quarter of 2026, versus $6.37 per barrel for the third quarter of 2025. Segment adjusted EBITDA excludes refining planned turnaround costs, which totaled $294 million in the third quarter of 2026 and $400 million in the third quarter of 2025. Results were driven primarily by higher market crack spreads in all regions.
R&M margin was $45.70 per barrel for the third quarter of 2026, versus $17.60 per barrel for the third quarter of 2025. Crude capacity utilization was 100%, resulting in total throughput of 3.2 million barrels per day (bpd) for the third quarter of 2026. Midstream
Segment adjusted EBITDA was $1.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, versus $1.7 billion for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by increased rates and throughputs, increased income from equity affiliates, and growth from acquisitions, partially offset by the divestiture of non-core gathering and processing assets as well as higher operating expenses, including incentive compensation.
Renewable Diesel
Segment adjusted EBITDA was $254 million in the third quarter of 2026, versus $(56) million for the third quarter of 2025. The results reflect a stronger margin environment, improved regulatory credit values, and higher throughputs.
Corporate and Items Not Allocated
Corporate expenses totaled $328 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $238 million in the third quarter of 2025. The variance was primarily due to higher expenses related to incentive compensation.
Financial Position, Liquidity, and Return of Capital
As of September 30, 2026, MPC had $13.6 billion of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, including $1.3 billion of cash at MPLX, and no borrowings outstanding under its $5 billion five-year bank revolving credit facility.

Strategic Update
MPC announced the divestiture of the St. Paul Park and Mandan refineries, inclusive of related midstream assets for $4.2 billion. MPC will receive $3.45 billion of the proceeds and intends to reinvest this capital prioritizing opportunities that meet the company’s return thresholds and advance its strategic priorities, primarily in the Gulf Coast. MPLX will receive $750 million of the proceeds, representing a 9x multiple on forecast 2027 adjusted EBITDA of the associated logistics assets. MPLX expects to redeploy this capital in support of its growth plans, anchored in the Permian and Marcellus basins. The transaction is expected to close at the end of 2026.
MPC’s 2026 capital spending outlook (excluding MPLX) is $1.5 billion. Approximately 65% of capital spending is focused on value-enhancing investments and 35% on sustaining operations. MPC’s outlook includes high-return investments at its Galveston Bay, Robinson, El Paso, and Garyville refineries. In addition to these multi-year investments, the company is executing shorter-term projects that offer high returns through margin enhancement and cost reduction.

About Marathon Petroleum Corporation
Marathon Petroleum Corporation (MPC) is a leading, integrated, downstream and midstream energy company headquartered in Findlay, Ohio. The company operates the nation’s largest refining system. MPC’s marketing system includes branded locations across the United States, including Marathon brand retail outlets. MPC also owns the general partner and majority limited partner interest in MPLX LP, a midstream company that owns and operates gathering, processing, and fractionation assets, as well as crude oil and light product transportation and logistics infrastructure. More information is available at www.marathonpetroleum.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Management uses certain financial measures to evaluate our operating performance that are calculated and presented on the basis of methodologies other than in accordance with GAAP. The non-GAAP financial measures we use are as follows:
Adjusted Net Income Attributable to MPC and Adjusted Diluted Income Per Share
Adjusted net income attributable to MPC is defined as net income attributable to MPC excluding the items in the table below, along with their related income tax effect. We have excluded these items because we believe that they are not indicative of our core operating performance. Adjusted diluted income per share is defined as adjusted net income attributable to MPC divided by the number of weighted-average shares outstanding in the applicable period, assuming dilution.
We believe the use of adjusted net income attributable to MPC and adjusted diluted income per share provides us and our investors with important measures of our ongoing financial performance to better assess our underlying business results and trends. Adjusted net income attributable to MPC or adjusted diluted income per share should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, net income attributable to MPC, diluted net income per share or any other measure of financial performance presented in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted net income attributable to MPC and adjusted diluted income per share may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies.

Adjusted EBITDA
Amounts included in net income (loss) attributable to MPC and excluded from adjusted EBITDA include (i) net interest and other financial costs; (ii) provision/benefit for income taxes; (iii) noncontrolling interests; (iv) depreciation and amortization; (v) refining planned turnaround costs and (vi) other adjustments as deemed necessary, as shown in the table below. We believe excluding turnaround costs from this metric is useful for comparability to other companies as certain of our competitors defer these costs and amortize them between turnarounds.
Adjusted EBITDA is a financial performance measure used by management, industry analysts, investors, lenders, and rating agencies to assess the financial performance and operating results of our ongoing business operations. Additionally, we believe adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors for trending, analyzing and benchmarking our operating results from period to period as compared to other companies that may have different financing and capital structures. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, income (loss) from operations, net income attributable to MPC, income before income taxes, cash flows from operating activities or any other measure of financial performance presented in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies.

This measure should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, Refining & Marketing gross margin or other measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP, and our calculation thereof may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies.
