EXHIBIT 99.1

Clearfield Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026 Results

  • Fiscal 2026 net sales from continuing operations of $151.9 million; Gross margin of 32.0%; Net income from continuing operations per diluted share of $0.16
  • Sales order backlog increased 75% to $43.3 million as of the end of fiscal 2026, compared to $24.7 million as of the end of the prior fiscal year 
  • Began shipments against the previously announced $22 million data center order, marking a significant milestone in the expansion of our data center business
  • Expects full year fiscal 2027 revenue of $195 million to $205 million, representing just over 30% growth from fiscal year 2026 revenue at the midpoint of guidance

(NASDAQ: CLFD), a leader in fiber connectivity solutions for telecom, data center, and network infrastructure environments, reported results for the fiscal fourth quarter and full year 2026. Additional commentary is provided in a letter to shareholders available in the Investor Relations section of the Company’s website.

Management Commentary

“Fiscal 2026 represented a pivotal transition year for Clearfield.  We continued serving our established broadband customers while expanding our fiber management expertise to the rapidly growing AI infrastructure market.  This last quarter, we have seen a positive sign in the Broadband market as Clearfield announced shipments to one of the first BEAD funded deployments here in Minnesota as well as the beginning of shipments to our first significant data center customer site.  We believe this demonstrates the work that has been accomplished to date and the significant growth we see in front of us,” said Cheri Beranek, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Since co-founding Clearfield in 2008, it has been a privilege to help build the Company alongside our dedicated team. As previously announced, I plan to retire effective March 31, 2027, or earlier upon the appointment of my successor. I will continue as President and CEO Emeritus to support the transition. I am proud of what we have accomplished and confident in Clearfield’s next phase of growth under new leadership.”

“Over the past three fiscal years, we have strengthened our financial position through disciplined balance sheet management, including strong cash generation and thoughtful capital allocation. During that period, we generated approximately $41 million in cumulative free cash flow and returned $65 million to shareholders through share repurchases. With $157 million in cash and investments and no debt, we have the flexibility to invest in our business and expand our addressable markets,” said Dan Herzog, Chief Financial Officer. "We are excited about the growth opportunities ahead in fiscal 2027, with revenue expected to be in the range of $195 million to $205 million, supported by an anticipated gradual recovery in broadband demand and the expected ramp-up of our data center business.”

Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026
Net sales from continuing operations for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 were $39.3 million, compared to $41.1 million in the prior year quarter.

As of September 30, 2026, order backlog (defined as purchase orders received but not yet fulfilled) was $43.3 million, an increase of $22.3 million, or 106%, compared to $21.0 million as of June 30, 2026, and an increase of $18.6 million, or 75%, from September 30, 2025.

Gross profit margin from continuing operations in the fourth quarter was 30.7%, compared with 34.6% in the prior year quarter and 31.8% in the third quarter.

Operating expenses from continuing operations for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 decreased to $13.1 million, or 33.3% of net sales, from $13.3 million, or 32.3% of net sales, in the same year-ago quarter.

Net loss from continuing operations for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 totaled $73,000, or $0.00 per diluted share, compared to net income of $1.8 million, or $0.13 per diluted share, in the same year-ago quarter. The Company repurchased approximately 60,000 shares for $1.8 million during the 3-month period ended September 30, 2026. There is approximately $13.2 million remaining for future repurchases as of September 30, 2026. 

Financial Results for the Year Ended September 30, 2026
Net sales from continuing operations were $151.9 million, compared to $150.1 million in fiscal year 2025. Performance for the quarter and full year reflected growth in the Large Regional segment, partly offset by softness in the MSO market.

Gross margin from continuing operations decreased from 33.7% in fiscal year 2025 to 32.0%. Gross profit margin for the fourth quarter and full year of fiscal 2026 was affected by higher product costs absorbed compared to prior periods, particularly in the latter part of the fiscal year.

Operating expenses from continuing operations for fiscal 2026 were $50.9 million, up 5% from $48.4 million in fiscal year 2025. As a percentage of net sales, operating expenses increased to 33.5%, from 32.3% in fiscal 2025.

Net income from continuing operations for fiscal 2026 was $2.1 million, down from $6.3 million in fiscal 2025. Earnings per diluted share from continuing operations decreased to $0.16 in fiscal 2026 from $0.45 per diluted share in fiscal 2025.

Outlook
For fiscal 2027, we expect net sales to be in the range of $195 million to $205 million, and net income per share to a range of $0.25 to $0.46. For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, we expect net sales in the range of $48 million to $52 million, operating expenses as a percentage of net sales to remain consistent with the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, and net income (loss) per share in the range of $(0.06) to $0.03. 

The net income (loss) per share ranges are based on the number of shares outstanding at the end of the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 and do not reflect the impact of any potential additional share repurchases completed in fiscal 2027. Our guidance also reflects our current expectations regarding the current data center environment and potential supply chain constraints of optical fiber, cable and connectors mentioned in our previous letters to shareholders, as well as our current understanding of the impact of the evolving tariff situation, both which could contribute to uncertainty in our business and in the macroeconomic environment.

About Clearfield, Inc.
Clearfield, Inc. (NASDAQ: CLFD) designs, manufactures, and distributes fiber optic management, protection, and delivery solutions that enable high-performance connectivity across a wide range of markets, helping to close the digital divide. Clearfield’s labor lite, craft-friendly platform is leveraged by community broadband providers, MSOs, incumbent service providers, ISPs, data centers, wireless providers, IoT networks, the military, municipalities, and cooperatives to connect homes, businesses and critical infrastructure faster and more efficiently. Headquartered in Minneapolis, MN, Clearfield deploys more than a million fiber ports each year. For more information, visit www.SeeClearfield.com.
