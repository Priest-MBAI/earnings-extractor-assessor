Rockwell Medical Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

•Net Sales for the third quarter 2026 were $19.1 million representing a 20% increase over the same period in 2025

•Gross Profit for the third quarter 2026 increased 37% over the same period in 2025 primarily driven by higher volumes

•The combination of Rockwell Medical with Vantive’s global hemodialysis concentrates, hemodialysis machines, water treatment systems, and related consumables and technical services business is projected to generate annual revenue between $400 million and $450 million

Wixom, Michigan, November 12, 2026 – Rockwell Medical, Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: RMTI), a healthcare company that develops, manufactures, commercializes and distributes a portfolio of hemodialysis products to dialysis providers worldwide, today announced financial and operational results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

“We have maintained solid upward momentum across our business, driven by robust market demand and disciplined execution,” said Mark Strobeck, Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer at Rockwell Medical. “Our third quarter 2026 net sales grew 20% year-over-year, reflecting strong customer adoption and market validation of our products. Our team’s commitment to quality and reliable customer service continues to set us apart. We are excited to build on this momentum with our recently announced equity and asset purchase agreement with Vantive, a transformational step that we believe will significantly expand our portfolio and global reach. As we celebrate our 30th anniversary, we are well-positioned to drive innovation and capitalize on emerging growth opportunities.”

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Net Sales

•Net sales for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $19.1 million, which represents a 20% increase over net sales of $15.9 million for the same period in 2025. The increase in net sales was driven by greater purchasing from our existing customers and additional sales to new customers in the western United States.

•Net sales for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $54.2 million, which represents a 7% increase over net sales of $50.9 million for the same period in 2025.

Gross Profit

•Gross profit for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $3.1 million, which represents a 37% increase over gross profit for the same period in 2025.

•Gross profit for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $9.3 million, which represents an 18% increase over gross profit of $7.8 million for the same period in 2025.



Gross Margin

•Gross margin for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was 16%, which represents an increase from 14% for the same period in 2025.

•Gross margin for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 17%, which represented an increase from 15% for the same period in 2025.

Net Loss

•Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $2.3 million compared to a net loss of $1.8 million for the same period in 2025.

•Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $5.1 million compared to a net loss of $4.8 million for the same period in 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA

•Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was ($0.3) million compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $50,000 for the same period in 2025.

•Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was ($0.7) million compared with Adjusted EBITDA of ($0.6) million for the same period in 2025.

Cash, Cash Equivalents and Investments Available-for-Sale

•Cash, cash equivalents and investments available-for-sale at September 30, 2026 was $22.5 million, which represents a decrease from $24.8 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease was primary driven by an increase in accounts receivable and prepaid expenses in the ordinary course of business, as well as capital purchases to further automate our manufacturing facilities.

OPERATING HIGHLIGHTS

•In July 2026, Rockwell Medical named Brian Cox as Vice President, Quality and Regulatory Compliance, advancing the Company’s quality and regulatory leadership. Mr. Cox brings over 20 years of medical device experience spanning development, manufacturing, and regulatory compliance. Having held leadership roles at global healthcare companies including Abbott and Johnson & Johnson, as well as smaller, growth-oriented medical device manufacturers, Mr. Cox has deep expertise in building and scaling quality systems in complex regulated environments.

•Subsequent to the end of the third quarter 2026, Great Place to Work® and Fortune magazine named Rockwell Medical to the 2026 Fortune Best Workplaces in Manufacturing & ProductionTM List in the small & medium category for the third year in a row.

•Subsequent to the end of the third quarter 2026, Rockwell Medical announced that it entered into an Equity and Asset Purchase Agreement (“the Agreement”) with Vantive Limited (“Vantive”). Subject to the terms of the Agreement, Rockwell Medical will acquire (the “Acquisition”) Vantive’s hemodialysis (“HD”) concentrates, HD machines including AK-98, water treatment systems, and related consumables and technical services business. In addition, subject to the terms and conditions of the Agreement, Rockwell Medical will acquire Vantive’s state-of-the art one million square foot manufacturing campus in Medolla, Italy, an integral municipality within the Mirandola Biomedical District, the third largest medtech region in the world. The Medolla campus currently manufactures dry concentrate cartridges, HD machines, related parts and consumables, and water treatment systems that support the broader HD product portfolio.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

To supplement Rockwell Medical’s unaudited condensed consolidated statements of operations and unaudited condensed consolidated balance sheets, which are prepared in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (“GAAP”), this press release also includes references to Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure that is defined as net income (loss) before net interest income (expense), net other income (expense), income tax expenses (benefit), depreciation and amortization, impairment charges, stock-based compensation expense, and other items that are considered unusual or not representative of underlying trends of our business, including but not limited to one-time severance costs, deferred revenue and inventory reserve amounts, if applicable for the periods presented. The Company has provided a reconciliation of net loss, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, to Adjusted EBITDA. In addition, this press release includes a reference to Adjusted EPS, a non-GAAP financial measure that is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by the weighted average number of shares outstanding. The Company has also provided a reconciliation to EPS, or net income divided by the weighted average number of shares outstanding, which is the most directly

comparable GAAP financial measure. Each of these adjusted measures is a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company has provided reconciliations to the GAAP measures at the end of this press release.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS are key measures used by Rockwell Medical to understand and evaluate operating performance and trends, to prepare and approve its annual budget and to develop short- and long-term operating plans. The Company provides Adjusted EBITDA because it believes the metric is helpful in highlighting trends in its operating results because it excludes items that are not indicative of Rockwell Medical’s core operating performance. In particular, the Company believes that the exclusion of the items eliminated in calculating Adjusted EBITDA provides useful measures for period-to-period comparisons of Rockwell Medical’s business. This is also true for Adjusted EPS, which is derived from Adjusted EBITDA.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS should not be considered in isolation of, or as an alternative to, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. Other companies, including companies in the same industry, may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS as tools for comparison. There are a number of limitations related to the use of these non-GAAP financial measures rather than the most directly comparable financial measure calculated in accordance with GAAP. When evaluating the Company’s performance, you should consider Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS alongside other financial performance measures, including net loss, EPS and other GAAP results.

ABOUT ROCKWELL MEDICAL

Rockwell Medical, Inc. (Nasdaq: RMTI) is a healthcare company that develops, manufactures, commercializes, and distributes a portfolio of hemodialysis products for dialysis providers worldwide. Rockwell Medical's mission is to provide dialysis clinics and the patients they serve with the highest quality products supported by the best customer service in the industry. Rockwell is focused on innovative, long-term growth strategies that enhance its products, its processes, and its people, enabling the Company to deliver exceptional value to the healthcare system and provide a positive impact on the lives of hemodialysis patients. Hemodialysis is the most common form of end-stage kidney disease treatment and is typically performed in freestanding outpatient dialysis centers, hospital-based outpatient centers, skilled nursing facilities, or a patient’s home. Rockwell Medical's products are vital to vulnerable patients with end-stage kidney disease, and the Company is relentless in providing unmatched reliability and customer service. Certified as a Great Place to Work® four years in a row (2023-2026) and named Fortune Best Workplaces in Manufacturing & Production™ three years in a row (2024-2026), Rockwell Medical is Driven to Deliver Life-Sustaining Dialysis Solutions™. For more information, visit www.rockwellmed.com.
