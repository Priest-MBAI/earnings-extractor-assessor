AGILYSYS ANNOUNCES RECORD REVENUE OF $93.4M IN FISCAL 2027 SECOND QUARTER

Inclusive of Subscription Revenue Growth of 32.6% and

Quarter Adjusted EBITDA of 25.9%

Raises Full-Year Fiscal 2027 Guidance Levels: Total Revenue Range to $374M to $378M and

Subscription Revenue Growth to At Least 34%

ALPHARETTA, GA – October 26, 2026 - Agilysys, Inc. (NASDAQ: AGYS), a leading global provider of hospitality software solutions and services, today reported results for its fiscal 2027 second quarter ending September 30, 2026.

Summary of Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Financial Results

•
Total net revenue increased 17.7% to a record $93.4 million compared to total net revenue of $79.3 million in the comparable prior-year period.
•
Recurring revenue (comprising subscription and maintenance charges) was a record $62.2 million, 66.6% of total net revenue, compared to $51.0 million, 64.3% of total net revenue for the same period in fiscal 2026. Subscription revenue increased 32.6% year-over-year and was 71.2% of total recurring revenue compared to 65.5% of total recurring revenue in the second quarter of fiscal 2026.
•
Gross margin was 64.6% in the fiscal 2027 second quarter compared to 61.7% in the comparable prior-year period.
•
Net income in the fiscal 2027 second quarter was $13.2 million, $0.46 per diluted share, compared to $11.7 million, $0.41 per diluted share, in the comparable prior-year period.
•
Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $24.1 million compared to $16.4 million in the comparable prior-year period (reconciliation included in financial tables).
•
Adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) was $0.67 per share in the fiscal 2027 second quarter compared to $0.40 per share in the comparable prior-year period (reconciliation included in financial tables).
•
Free cash flow (non-GAAP) in the fiscal 2027 second quarter was $14.8 million compared to free cash flow of $15.0 million in the fiscal 2026 second quarter (reconciliation included in financial tables). Ending cash balance was $137.6 million compared to ending cash balance of $116.9 million as of fiscal 2026 year-end.

Ramesh Srinivasan, President and CEO of Agilysys, commented, “Revenue was a record $93.4 million during Q2 Fiscal 2027, the 19th consecutive record revenue quarter, driven by subscription growth of 32.6%.

Q2 Fiscal 2027 one-time product revenue was higher than expected as the hospitality industry continues to increase its investments in modern point of sale (POS) technology solutions. The industry also continues to move forward in earnest to implement the modern, cloud-native ecosystem of software solutions that allows them to improve operational efficiencies and deliver great guest experiences. Q2 Fiscal 2027 was yet another record sales quarter, the best ever Q2 July to September sales period, keeping total backlog at record levels despite strong implementation and delivery results.

The business continues to grow ahead of our previous expectations. As a result, we are once again raising full fiscal year total annual revenue guidance to now be in the range of $374 million to $378 million, driven among other things, by an increase in subscription revenue growth, now expected to increase by at least 34% year over year. We expect full fiscal year Adjusted EBITDA to be 25% of revenue, higher than the prior expectation of 24%,” Srinivasan concluded.

Fiscal 2027 Outlook

The Company is raising full year subscription revenue growth guidance of at least 32% year-over-year to at least 34% and full year fiscal 2027 total revenue to be $374 million to $378 million. Adjusted EBITDA expectations are also increasing to 25% of revenue for the full fiscal year.

Dave Wood, Chief Financial Officer, commented, “The second quarter of the fiscal year has continued to yield strong results. The teams are continuing to execute at elevated levels across the board, driven by a sustained high level of sales demand

 

and project implementation velocity. We are pleased to be in a position once again to increase our full year expectations as a result of this continued momentum. The company is well positioned to continue to execute against the current forecasted plan during the second half of the fiscal year.”

About Agilysys

Agilysys delivers state-of-the-art software and services built exclusively for hospitality, helping organizations go beyond traditional property management (PMS), point-of-sale (POS), and food and beverage inventory and procurement systems. Its best-in-class solutions operate independently or as a unified ecosystem that connects data and workflows across an operation, property or enterprise. Powered by intelligent guest profiles, data-driven capabilities, and embedded artificial intelligence (AI), Agilysys is architected on a unified data model with a single set of APIs that create shared operational memory across all systems, enabling guest-specific insights to be acted on in real time at any touchpoint. Agilysys delivers advantage through pace and integration, transforming hospitality technology from a system of record into a system of action that accelerates innovation and the rapid delivery of new capabilities. Agilysys serves a 100% hospitality customer base, including hotels and resorts, casinos, cruise lines, corporate and campus dining, healthcare, senior living, and entertainment venues worldwide. Agilysys operates across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific, and India with headquarters located in Alpharetta, GA. For more information visit Agilysys.com.
