Vivos Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Operational Update

 

Revenue rises 24% quarter over quarter as Third quarter operating loss improves 18% year over year on cost reductions; Company regained compliance with Nasdaq stockholders’ equity requirement

 

Management to Host Conference Call today at 5:00 pm ET

 

LITTLETON, Colo., November 16, 2026 – Vivos Therapeutics, Inc. (“Vivos” or the “Company’’) (NASDAQ: VVOS), a leading medical device and healthcare services company focused on developing and commercializing innovative diagnostic and treatment modalities for patients suffering from obstructive sleep apnea (“OSA”) and other sleep related breathing disorders, today reported financial results and operating highlights for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Kirk Huntsman, Vivos’ Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “During the third quarter of 2026, we took decisive steps to reduce costs and strengthen our balance sheet. Our operating loss improved 18% compared to the prior-year quarter, general and administrative and sales and marketing expenses together declined by $1.6 million, and we have now eliminated an estimated $5 million or more in annual operating costs, with further efficiencies underway. We also exchanged approximately $6.1 million of senior secured debt for equity during 2026 and, following the closing of our SOD Sciences transaction in October, regained compliance with Nasdaq’s stockholders’ equity requirement.”

 

Mr. Huntsman continued, “We are also pursuing a number of clinical initiatives at our SCN and SAMC centers, including EEG testing and treatment for insomnia and other non-OSA sleep disorders, the distribution and set-up of PAP and CPAP treatments, and remote patient monitoring of CPAP users, together with the renegotiation of our payer contracts. We expect these initiatives to contribute to revenue and margins beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026 and increasingly throughout 2027.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Summary

 

 

●

Revenue was $6.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, up 24% from $5.2 million in the second quarter of 2026, and $16.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.8 million and $13.6 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025. Third quarter service revenue increased 1% to $4.6 million, while product revenue decreased to $1.8 million from $2.2 million, reflecting our strategic pivot away from appliance sales to dentists in our Vivos Integrated Provider (VIP) program. For the nine months, service revenue increased 57% to $12.1 million, primarily attributable to sleep testing and resulting treatment services in our Nevada operations;

     
  ● Gross profit was $3.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 and $9.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $3.9 million and $7.6 million for the comparable periods in 2025. The third quarter decrease was due to lower product revenue and higher cost of sales, while the nine-month increase was due to stronger service revenue;
     
  ● Gross margin was 52% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 58% in the third quarter of 2025, due to the decrease in product revenue. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, gross margin was 56% compared to 55% in the same period in 2025;
     
  ● Operating expenses for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $7.2 million, a 17% decrease compared to $8.7 million in the same period a year ago, reflecting a $1.4 million reduction in general and administrative expenses, driven primarily by lower professional fees, equipment maintenance and office expenses, as well as lower sales and marketing expenses. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, operating expenses were $24.7 million versus $21.1 million in the same period in 2025, an increase driven primarily by the costs of establishing our two Sleep and Airway Medicine Centers in Las Vegas and Henderson, Nevada;

 

 
 

 

 

●

The third quarter 2026 operating loss improved 18% to $3.9 million, compared to $4.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the operating loss was $15.3 million compared to $13.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

     
  ● Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $2.0 million, compared to $5.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, and included approximately $2.9 million of other income from the reversal of our previously recorded Employee Retention Credit liability;
     
  ● At September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $0.2 million and total stockholders’ equity was $2.4 million, compared to cash and cash equivalents of $2.0 million and a stockholders’ deficit of $1.5 million as of December 31, 2025. Total liabilities decreased to $20.7 million at September 30, 2026 from $26.7 million at December 31, 2025. Subsequent to quarter end, on October 16, 2026, we completed our SOD Sciences transaction, and on October 20, 2026, Nasdaq notified us that we had regained compliance with its minimum stockholders’ equity requirement, subject to Nasdaq’s continued monitoring.

About Vivos Therapeutics, Inc.

 

Vivos Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: VVOS) is a medical technology and healthcare services company focused on developing and commercializing innovative diagnostic and treatment methods for patients suffering from breathing and sleep issues arising from certain dentofacial abnormalities such as obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and snoring in adults. Vivos’ devices have been cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for adult patients diagnosed with all severity levels of OSA and moderate-to-severe OSA in children ages 6 to 17. Vivos’ groundbreaking Complete Airway Repositioning and Expansion (CARE) devices were the first oral appliances to receive FDA 510(k) clearance for treating severe OSA in adults and the flagship DNA appliance is the first oral appliance to receive FDA clearance for treating moderate to severe OSA in children.

 

OSA and insomnia affect over 1 billion adults aged 30-69 years old worldwide, yet 80% or more remain undiagnosed and unaware of their condition. These chronic disorders are not just a sleep issue - they are closely linked to many serious chronic health conditions. While the medical community has made strides in treating sleep disorders, breathing and sleep health remain areas that are still not fully understood. As a result, legacy OSA treatments like CPAP are often mechanistic and fail to address the root causes. Vivos CARE oral medical devices target the underlying physiology and anatomical deficiencies that are often associated with OSA.

 

Through innovative technology, education, and acquisitions of, or commercial collaborations with, sleep healthcare providers, Vivos is empowering healthcare providers to address the complex needs of OSA patients more thoroughly.

 

Vivos calls the use of its appliances and protocols to treat OSA The Vivos Method, which offers a proprietary, clinically effective solution that is nonsurgical, noninvasive, and nonpharmaceutical, providing hope to allow patients to Breathe New Life.
