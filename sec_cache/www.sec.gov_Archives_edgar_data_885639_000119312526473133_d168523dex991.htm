Kohl’s Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Results

MENOMONEE FALLS, Wis.—(BUSINESS WIRE)—November 24, 2026— Kohl’s Corporation (NYSE: KSS) (“Kohl’s” or the “Company”) today reported results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026.

 

  •  

Net sales increased 1.3% and comparable sales increased 1.2%

 

  •  

Gross margin increased 145 basis points

 

  •  

Tax benefit of $137 million or $1.16 per diluted share recognized in the quarter

 

  •  

Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) of $2.15 for the quarter

 

  •  

Includes gain on sale of real estate of $61 million or $0.39 per diluted share

 

  •  

Includes tax benefit of $137 million or $1.16 per diluted share

 

  •  

Includes tariff refund benefit of $45 million or $0.28 per diluted share

 

  •  

Raises full year 2026 financial outlook

Michael Bender, Kohl’s Chief Executive Officer, said, “In the third quarter, we returned to top-line growth with a positive 1.2% comparable sales performance. As we make steady, tangible progress on our strategic initiatives, our customers are responding favorably, driving broad-based improvements across the business. While this is an encouraging milestone, we know crucial work remains ahead.

Alongside the sales performance, our team operated with strong financial discipline, optimizing our cost structure, solidifying our balance sheet, and delivering strong cash generation. This operational rigor gives us the financial flexibility and confidence to continue investing in the business and deliver sustainable long-term shareholder value,” Bender continued.

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Comparisons refer to the 13-week period ended October 31, 2026 versus the 13-week period ended November 1, 2025

 

  •  

Net sales increased 1.3% year-over-year, to $3.5 billion, with comparable sales also up 1.2%.

 

  •  

Gross margin as a percentage of net sales was 41.0%, an increase of 145 basis points year-over-year.

 

  •  

Selling, general & administrative (SG&A) expenses increased 1.2% year-over-year, to $1.3 billion. As a percentage of total revenue, SG&A expenses were 35.1%, a decrease of 20 basis points year-over-year.

 

  •  

Operating income was $217 million compared to $73 million in the prior year. As a percentage of total revenue, operating income was 6.0%, an increase of 392 basis points year-over-year. Adjusted operating income was $156 million compared to $77 million in the prior year. As a percentage of total revenue, adjusted operating income was 4.3%, an increase of 213 basis points year-over-year.(a)

 

  •  

Net income was $254 million, or $2.15 per diluted share. This compares to net income of $8 million, or $0.07 per diluted share in the prior year. Adjusted net income was $207 million, or $1.76 per adjusted diluted share. This compares to adjusted net income of $11 million, or $0.10 per adjusted diluted share, in the prior year.(a)

 

  •  

Inventory was $3.9 billion, a decrease of 1% year-over-year.

 

  •  

Tax benefit of $137 million resulting from tax examinations being effectively settled was recorded during the quarter, which increased diluted EPS by $1.16 for the quarter.

 

  •  

Tariff refunds of approximately $45 million flowed through gross margin in the quarter.

 

  •  

Gain on sale of real estate of $61 million was recognized related to the sale of two properties.

Nine Months Fiscal Year 2026 Results

Comparisons refer to the 39-week period ended October 31, 2026 versus the 39-week period ended November 1, 2025

 

  •  

Net sales decreased 0.4% year-over-year, to $9.8 billion, with comparable sales also down 0.2%.

 

  •  

Gross margin as a percentage of net sales was 41.3%, an increase of 156 basis points year-over-year.

 

  •  

Selling, general & administrative expenses decreased 0.4% year-over-year to $3.6 billion. As a percentage of total revenue, SG&A expenses were 35.0%, a decrease of 2 basis points year-over-year.

 

  •  

Operating income was $524 million compared to $412 million in the prior year. As a percentage of total revenue, operating income was 5.1%, an increase of 110 basis points year-over-year. Adjusted operating income was $463 million compared to $298 million in the prior year. As a percentage of total revenue, adjusted operating income was 4.5%, an increase of 160 basis points year-over-year.(a)

  •  

Net income was $391 million, or $3.33 per diluted share. This compares to net income of $147 million, or $1.30 per diluted share in the prior year. Adjusted net income was $344 million, or $2.94 per adjusted diluted share. This compares to adjusted net income of $61 million, or $0.54 per adjusted diluted share, in the prior year.(a)

 

  •  

Long-term debt decreased $196 million compared to the prior year, primarily driven by $113 million of debt repurchased at a discount of $15 million in 2026 and $87 million of debt repurchases in the prior year.

 

  •  

Tax benefit of $137 million resulting from tax examinations being effectively settled was recorded during the year, which increased diluted EPS by $1.17 for the year.

 

  •  

Tariff refunds of approximately $190 million were received, of which approximately $145 million flowed through gross margin.

 

  •  

Gain on sale of real estate of $61 million was recognized related to the sale of two properties.

 

(a)

Non-GAAP financial measures: Please see the “RECONCILIATION OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES” for a reconciliation of adjusted operating income to operating income, adjusted net income to net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per share to diluted earnings per share.

2026 Financial and Capital Allocation Outlook

The Company raises its guidance. For the full year 2026, the Company now expects the following GAAP outlook:

 

  •  

Net sales and Comparable sales: In the range of flat to 0.5%

 

  •  

Operating margin: In the range of 4.7% to 4.9% which includes the benefit of:

 

  •  

0.4% from gain on real estate

 

  •  

1.0% from tariff refunds

 

  •  

Diluted EPS: In the range of $4.30 to $4.50 which includes the benefit of:

 

  •  

$0.40 from gain on real estate

 

  •  

$1.03 from tariff refunds

 

  •  

$1.17 from the tax benefit

 

  •  

Capital Expenditures: In the range of $365 million to $375 million

 

  •  

Dividend: On November 18, 2026, Kohl’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend on the Company’s common stock of $0.125 per share. The dividend is payable December 23, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 9, 2026.

 

  •  

Share Repurchase Program: Planning share repurchases of up to $100 million in 2026 under existing $3 billion authorization

Fourth Quarter 2026 Financial Outlook

Based on the updated full year outlook, The Company expects the following GAAP outlook for the fourth quarter:

 

  •  

Net sales and Comparable sales: In the range of flat to up 2.0%

 

  •  

Operating margin: In the range of 4.0% to 4.4% which includes the benefit of:

 

  •  

approximately 0.25% from tariff refunds

 

  •  

Diluted EPS: In the range of $0.95 to $1.15 which includes the benefit of:

 

  •  

approximately $0.10 from tariff refunds

About Kohl’s

Kohl’s (NYSE: KSS) is a leading omnichannel retailer built on a foundation that combines great brands, incredible value and convenience for our customers. Kohl’s is uniquely positioned to deliver against its long-term strategy and its purpose to take care of families’ realest moments. Kohl’s serves millions of families in its more than 1,100 stores in 49 states, online at Kohls.com, and through the Kohl’s App. With a large national footprint, Kohl’s is committed to making a positive impact in the communities it serves.
