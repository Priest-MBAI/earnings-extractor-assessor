MESSAGE FROM OUR CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT & CEO
Busey reported adjusted diluted EPS2 of $0.63. Results were impacted by a single commercial relationship in the Central region that incurred a partial charge-off and moved to non-accrual this quarter. Quarterly provision expense increased to $9.6 million, compared to $2.2 million in the prior quarter, primarily as a result of this credit.

Excluding this relationship, underlying trends for the quarter remained solid. Operating revenue2 was stable at $194.3 million, supported by growth in net interest income. Wealth management revenue increased 15.5% from the prior year. We also maintained strong expense discipline, producing an efficiency ratio2 of 54.4%.

Deposit growth remained a highlight, increasing at an annualized rate of 11% for the second consecutive quarter as we continued to add relationships and deepen client primacy across our markets. As anticipated, loan balances declined during the quarter, including the repositioning of approximately $65 million of lender finance loans to held for sale as part of our ongoing portfolio management efforts. The loan-to-deposit ratio improved to 82.5% from 87.2% last quarter.

Capital remained strong with Common Equity Tier 1 Capital to Risk Weighted Assets3 rising to 12.5% (compared to 12.3% a year ago), even after $188.4 million in year-to-date share repurchases, including $59.6 million this quarter.

As we enter the final quarter of 2026, we remain well positioned with an indomitable balance sheet, ample liquidity, and significant capital flexibility. While macroeconomic uncertainty persists, we are confident in our ability to navigate a range of operating environments while continuing to support our clients and create long-term value for our shareholders.
Van A. Dukeman
Chairman, President and CEO of First Busey Corporation

Net Interest Income
Net interest income increased by $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to the second quarter of 2026, driven by growth in average earning assets. Deposit funding costs were 13 basis points higher during the quarter largely due to continued success in deposit gathering initiatives as well as repricing impacts from the late quarter Federal Open Market Committee rate hike. Based on our most recent Asset Liability Management Committee model, a +100 basis point parallel rate shock is expected to increase net interest income by 1.5% (relative to a current base rate scenario) over the subsequent twelve-month period. Busey continues to evaluate and execute off-balance sheet hedging and balance sheet strategies as well as embedding rate protection in our asset originations to provide consistent and predictable net interest income performance across different interest rate environments. Deposit balances increased by $408.8 million, or 2.7%, primarily as a result of seasonal public funds inflows and strategic efforts to grow core customer deposits. At September 30, 2026, Busey Bank had $10.0 million of brokered funding, comprising 0.1% of total deposits, reflecting a $50.0 million reduction of brokered funding during the third quarter. Total deposit cost of funds increased from 1.80% during the second quarter of 2026 to 1.93% during the third quarter of 2026, with spot rates on total deposit costs increasing from 1.87% at June 30, 2026, to 1.97% at September 30, 2026. Busey continues to operate in a net excess cash position and at September 30, 2026, had cash and due from banks balances of $907.5 million. Busey’s average total cost of funds was 1.99% for the third quarter of 2026.
