F&M BANK CORP. REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 EARNINGS AND QUARTERLY DIVIDEND

 

Financial results from operations remain strong for the

 three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026.

 

See associated, unaudited summary consolidated financial data for additional information.

 

Timberville, VA / October 28, 2026 . . . F&M Bank Corp. (the “Company” or “F&M”), (OTCQX: FMBM), the parent company of Farmers & Merchants Bank (“F&M Bank” or the “Bank”), today reports earnings for the three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026.

 

Net income for third quarter 2026 was $2.64 million, or $0.74 per diluted share, compared to net income reported for second quarter 2026 of $5.37 million, or $1.50 per diluted share, and net income reported for third quarter 2025 of $2.92 million, or $0.82 per diluted share. For the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026, net income was $11.24 million, or $3.14 per diluted share, exceeding net income of $8.34 million, or $2.35 per diluted share, reported for the same period in 2025.

 

At September 30, 2026, the Company had total assets of $1.4 billion, total loans held for investment of $970.2 million, and total deposits of $1.28 billion. This reflects growth of $45.7 million, or 3.33%, in total assets; $83.9 million, or 9.47%, in total loans; and $36.4 million, or 2.92%, in total deposits over totals reported at December 31, 2025. During third quarter 2026, total loans grew by $44.9 million, or 4.85%, and total deposits grew by $24.1 million, or 1.92%.

 

“Our financial performance for 2026 continues to be strong,” said CEO Mike Wilkerson. “These results reflect a number of key factors: the F&M Team is extraordinary in their commitment, their efforts, and their energy in delivering exceptional customer service, expanding current relationships, and establishing new relationships. Their resolve has a meaningful impact on families, farmers, and businesses in the communities we serve. As a result, our diversified lines of business are all experiencing positive results year to date.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 INCOME STATEMENT REVIEW

 

Net Interest Income

Net interest income totaled $12.29 million for third quarter 2026, a decrease of $38,000, or 0.31%, on a linked-quarter basis and an increase of $1.80 million, or 17.14%, year-to-year. The linked‑quarter decrease was driven by a $369,000 increase in interest income offset by an increase in interest expense of $407,000. Interest income grew from second to third quarter, driven by the increase in average balance of loans held for investment, even with the impact of a second quarter recovery of interest and fees on the payout of a nonaccrual loan. The linked quarter increase in interest expense is the result of growth in time deposits during the quarter.

 

The year‑over‑year increase was primarily the result of loan growth, which contributed to a $1.42 million increase in interest income, along with a $379,000 decrease in interest expense. The decrease in interest expense in the year-over-year comparison was driven by a shift from higher-cost time deposits to lower-cost interest checking and money market accounts.

 

Net interest margin decreased to 3.69%, compared to 3.79% for second quarter 2026 and 3.36% for third quarter 2025. In second quarter 2026, the recovery of interest on a paid out non-accrual loan increased the earning asset yield and net interest margin by 6 basis points. Yield on earning assets was 5.54%, compared to 5.56% on a linked-quarter basis and 5.45% for the same quarter last year. Cost of funds was 1.92%, an increase of 9 basis points quarter-to-quarter and a decrease of 22 basis points year-to-year.

 

Provision for Credit Losses

The Bank recorded a provision for credit losses of $1.53 million during third quarter 2026, compared to a provision of $269,000 in second quarter 2026 and a provision of $539,000 in third quarter 2025. The third quarter 2026 provision included a $1.54 million provision for loans held for investment and a $9,000 recovery of reserve for unfunded loan commitments. The increase in the provision when compared to both historical periods was driven by qualitative factor updates to the allowance model, as well as loan portfolio growth of $44.91 million during the third quarter and $83.92 million year-to-date.

 

Nonperforming loans were $4.19 million at the end of the quarter, a $242,000 increase during the quarter and a $3.27 million decrease from September 30, 2025. Net charge-offs for the quarter were $410,000 or 0.17%, higher than 0.06% for second quarter 2026 and lower than 0.49% for third quarter 2025. Year-to-date net charge-offs were $738,000, $1.04 million lower than for the same period in 2025.

 

Noninterest Income

Total noninterest income was $2.93 million for third quarter 2026, compared to $4.85 million on a linked-quarter basis and $2.73 million year-to-year. The $1.9 million decrease from second quarter 2026 is primarily attributable to the $4.8 million gain on the sale of Richmond-based Bearing Insurance, which was partially offset by the $3.5 million loss on the restructuring of the Bank’s available for sale (“AFS”) securities portfolio. The $196,000 increase from third quarter 2025 resulted from a volume-related increase in title insurance income.

Noninterest Expenses

Noninterest expenses totaled $10.67 million for third quarter 2026, compared to $10.45 million on a linked-quarter basis and $9.32 million year-to-year. The $225,000 increase compared to the linked quarter was primarily driven by increased compensation expense, partially offset by declines in ATM and check card fees and legal and professional fees. The $1.4 million increase compared to a year ago was primarily attributable to increased compensation expense, legal and professional fees, and other operating expenses, and was partially offset by a reduction in ATM and check card fees.

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 BALANCE SHEET REVIEW

 

Total assets were $1.42 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of $45.7 million, or 3.33%, from December 31, 2025. Loans held for investment increased by $83.9 million, or 9.47%, to $970.2 million for the same period. Loan growth since year-end 2025 was driven by increases of $31.1 million in residential mortgage loans, $7.1 million in multifamily loans, $9.2 million in home equity loans, $12.3 million in commercial and industrial loans, $28.8 million in nonowner‑occupied commercial real estate loans, $13.7 million in loans secured by farmland, and $9.2 million in other construction and land development loans. These increases were partially offset by decreases of $9.2 million in residential construction loans, $5.2 million in owner‑occupied commercial real estate loans, and $13.6 million in automobile loans. All remaining loan categories increased by a net $468,000.

 

Investment securities decreased by $34.0 million, or 9.9%, since December 31, 2025, to $311.3 million at the end of third quarter 2026. The decrease reflected sales of $29.8 million and purchases of $49.0 million, including the restructuring of the AFS securities portfolio in June 2026. The average yield on the bond purchases in the first nine months of 2026 was 4.91%. Other changes included $37.0 million in paydowns of mortgage-backed securities, $12.9 million in maturities and calls, net premium amortization of $114,000, and a $3.3 million increase in unrealized losses within the securities portfolio. Total unrealized loss on the portfolio increased to $24.3 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $21.0 million at December 31, 2025.

 

Total deposits were $1.28 billion on September 30, 2026, an increase of $36.4 million, or 2.92%, from December 31, 2025. The increase reflected growth of $792,000, or 0.28%, in noninterest‑bearing deposits and $35.6 million, or 3.68%, in interest‑bearing deposits during the first nine months of the year.

 

Shareholders’ equity increased by $6.4 million, or 6.18% during the first nine months of the year, to $110.4 million at quarter end. The increase was driven by $11.2 million in net income, $312,000 related to shares issued, $437,000 in stock‑based compensation expense, partially offset by $2.8 million in dividends paid, $2.6 million in other comprehensive losses, and $136,000 in net vesting of restricted stock. Book value per share increased from $29.49 at December 31, 2025, to $31.06 at September 30, 2026, while tangible book value per share1 increased from $28.60 to $30.18 over the same period.

LIQUIDITY

 

On‑balance sheet liquid assets, including cash and cash equivalents, unpledged investment securities, and loans held for sale, totaled $281.7 million on September 30, 2026, representing 19.86% of total assets, compared to $289.0 million, or 21.05% of total assets, at December 31, 2025.

 

In addition to on‑balance sheet liquidity, the Bank had access to significant off‑balance sheet liquidity sources. As of September 30, 2026, available unsecured federal funds lines totaled $90.0 million. The Bank also maintained a secured line of credit with the Federal Home Loan Bank with available borrowing capacity of $214.4 million, secured by a blanket lien on qualifying loans. The Bank pledged securities with a collateral value of $88.8 million to the Federal Reserve Bank discount window, which may be used for overnight borrowings.

 

LOAN PORTFOLIO

 

The Company’s loan portfolio remains diversified. Residential mortgage loans totaled $280.4 million on September 30, 2026, representing 28.91% of total loans. Total commercial real estate loans, including both owner‑occupied and nonowner‑occupied properties, totaled $234.7 million, or 24.19% of total loans. Loans secured by farmland totaled $128.7 million or 13.27% of total loans. The outstanding balance of automobile loans originated through the dealer finance division totaled $63.5 million, representing 6.55% of the loan portfolio.

 

Additional details regarding loan portfolio composition as of September 30, 2026, and for the preceding four quarters are provided under the heading “Loan Data” in the tables accompanying this release.

 

ASSET QUALITY AND ALLOWANCE FOR CREDIT LOSSES

 

Asset quality metrics remained solid during third quarter 2026. Nonperforming loans as a percentage of total loans remained static as of September 30, 2026, and June 30, 2026, at 0.43%, declining from 0.68% as of December 31, 2025. Net charge‑offs as a percentage of average loans were 0.17% for third quarter 2026, compared to 0.06% for second quarter 2026 and 0.49% for third quarter 2025.

 

The allowance for credit losses on loans (“ACLL”) totaled $9.2 million at September 30, 2026, an increase of $1.4 million, or 17.91%, from December 31, 2025. The ACLL as a percentage of loans increased by 7 basis points to 0.95% of loans held for investment from the end of 2025 to September 30, 2026. The increase in the allowance reflected overall growth in loans held for investment of $83.9 million during the period and a change in the qualitative factors used in the allowance model. The reserve for unfunded loan commitments totaled $740,000 as of September 30, 2026, compared to $766,000 as of December 31, 2025 as a result of changes in unused commitments from one period to the next.

Based on our most recent trade price of $40.90 per share, this constitutes a 2.54% yield on an annualized basis. The dividend will be paid on November 30, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 14, 2026.

ABOUT US

F&M Bank Corp. is an independent, publicly traded, financial holding company offering a full range of financial services through our subsidiary, Farmers & Merchants Bank’s (F&M Bank), fourteen banking offices in Rockingham, Shenandoah, and Augusta counties, Virginia, and the cities of Winchester and Waynesboro, Virginia. The Company also owns VSTitle, a title company subsidiary. Founded in 1908 to serve the farmers and merchants of the Shenandoah Valley, where both the Company and the Bank are headquartered, F&M Bank is committed to the success of the agricultural industry, small business ventures, and the nonprofit sector.F&M’s values, which are gregarious, resolute, original, and wholehearted (G.R.O.W.), combined with our brand pillars of sustenance, security, and enrichment, shape the Company’s decision-making, philanthropy, and volunteerism. The only publicly traded organization based in Rockingham County, we offer a diverse suite of financial products and services, and a strong team dedicated to living our mission of being the financial partner of choice in the Shenandoah Valley both today and tomorrow, as we have been since 1908. Additional information may be found by visiting our website: fmbankva.com.
