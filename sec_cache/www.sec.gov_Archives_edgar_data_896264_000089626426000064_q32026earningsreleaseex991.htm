USANA Health Sciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Hiya Active Monthly Subscribers Increase 18% Sequentially; Core Nutritional Operating Margin Expands; Company Generates $36 Million in Year-to-Date Free Cash Flow

SALT LAKE CITY, November 3, 2026 (BUSINESS WIRE)—USANA Health Sciences, Inc. (NYSE: USNA) today announced financial results for its fiscal third quarter ended October 3, 2026.

Key Financial Results
Third Quarter 2026 vs. Third Quarter 2025
•Net sales of $206 million versus $214 million.
•Net loss of $(5.6) million versus $(6.5) million.
•Diluted EPS of $(0.30) as compared with $(0.36).
•Adjusted diluted EPS(1) of $(0.11) as compared with $(0.15).
•Adjusted EBITDA(2) of $12.1 million versus $13.8 million.
•Core Nutritional Active Customers of 356,000 versus 388,000.
•Hiya Active Monthly Subscribers of 195,500 versus 193,400.
•Company updates fiscal 2026 guidance to net sales approaching $900 million and Adjusted EBITDA(2) of $77 million.

“As we continue our transformation to an omnichannel health and wellness company, our third quarter results were mixed, with meaningful improvement in some areas and results that fell short of our expectations in others,” said Kevin Guest, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “We are investing across each of our businesses to strengthen their long-term competitive position and made clear progress in key areas. At Hiya, as customer acquisition began responding positively during the quarter, we increased marketing spend to build on that traction, which pressured margins but generated an 18% sequential increase in active subscribers and returned the segment to year-over-year net sales growth. Additionally, our Core Nutritional business expanded its operating margin from a year ago on a leaner cost structure despite lower net sales. While we have more work to do in growing Core Nutritional active customers and net sales at Rise Wellness, we believe we have the right strategies in place.

“In Core Nutritional, rebuilding our active customer base is our top priority and our single largest growth lever. For example, active customers in the Southeast Asia Pacific region grew 3% sequentially in the third quarter, demonstrating what works when our initiatives and execution align. We are evolving our Brand Partner incentive plan to more directly reward growth in active customers, investing in digital marketing to build awareness of the USANA brand, and advancing product innovation that we plan to begin introducing in 2027.

“At Hiya, our investment in customer acquisition is producing encouraging early returns. Notably, Hiya added approximately 29,500 net active monthly subscribers sequentially, customer acquisition costs declined noticeably from the first half of the year and net sales through Target and Amazon more than doubled from the second quarter. Because Hiya is predominantly a subscription business today, the subscribers we add support results in future quarters. Additionally, Hiya continues to make progress in broadening its reach beyond direct-to-consumer, and we look forward to providing updates in early 2027.



“At Rise Wellness, we are building on a strengthening retail foundation and working towards improved margins. As planned, we launched Protein Pop Balance nationally at Target and online in September, and Target sales grew strongly from the second quarter. Our focus is converting this progress into consistent, profitable growth.”

Balance Sheet
The Company ended the quarter with $179 million in cash and cash equivalents and zero debt. As of October 3, 2026, inventory totaled $93 million, a decrease of approximately $14 million, or 13%, compared to balances at year-end 2025.

The Company did not repurchase any shares during the quarter and has approximately $34 million remaining under the current share repurchase authorization as of the end of the third quarter.

“Consolidated net sales declined 4% in the third quarter, as growth at Hiya was more than offset by lower sales in Core Nutritional and Rise,” said Doug Hekking, Chief Financial Officer. “We made progress on improving profitability in our Core Nutritional segment during the quarter, while we chose to increase investment at Hiya to accelerate subscriber growth, which weighed on margins in the quarter but produced strong growth in active monthly subscribers.

“We generated $8 million of free cash flow(3) in the quarter and $36 million year-to-date, reduced inventory 13% from year-end, and ended the period with $179 million in cash and no debt. That financial strength gives us the flexibility to continue investing in the initiatives we expect to drive future growth across our businesses, including our omnichannel strategy, while maintaining financial discipline.

“We are updating our fiscal 2026 outlook to consolidated net sales approaching $900 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $77 million. Compared with our prior outlook, as corrected, the change reflects lower expectations for Rise Wellness and our deliberate decision to invest resources at Hiya to drive increased active monthly subscribers, partially offset by stronger profitability in Core Nutritional. We expect continued


sequential progress from Hiya during the fourth quarter on both net sales and Adjusted EBITDA. While these investments constrain near-term results, we believe they set the stage for renewed growth across our businesses, and we plan to enter 2027 with a strong balance sheet and clear priorities to deliver it.”

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release contains the non-GAAP financial measures Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Diluted EPS and free cash flow. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure of (loss) earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization that also excludes certain adjustments as indicated below in the reconciliation from net (loss) earnings. Adjusted Diluted EPS is a non-GAAP financial measure of diluted (loss) earnings per share that excludes certain adjustments as indicated below in the reconciliation from diluted EPS.

Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) is net (loss) earnings (its most directly comparable GAAP financial measure) adjusted for interest expense, net, (benefit from) provision for income taxes, depreciation and amortization (including right-of-use asset reduction), non-cash share-based compensation, transaction-related expenses and integration costs for the Hiya acquisition, cost realignment expenses, impairment expense, and gain on sale of assets. Adjusted EBITDA attributable to USANA (non-GAAP) is Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) further adjusted to exclude the Adjusted EBITDA attributable to noncontrolling interest related to Hiya.

Adjusted diluted (loss) earnings per share (non-GAAP) is diluted (loss) earnings per share (its most directly comparable GAAP financial measure) adjusted for amortization of intangible assets, transaction-related expenses and integration costs related to the Hiya acquisition, cost realignment expenses, impairment expense, and gain on sale of assets.

Free cash flow (non-GAAP) is net cash provided by (used in) operating activities (its most directly comparable GAAP financial measure) less purchases of property and


equipment. Management believes free cash flow is a useful measure of the cash the business generates, after investing in property and equipment, that is available for strategic investments, share repurchases and other uses.

Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP), Adjusted EBITDA attributable to USANA (non-GAAP), Adjusted diluted (loss) earnings per share (non-GAAP), and free cash flow (non-GAAP), along with GAAP measures used by management, most appropriately reflect how the Company measures the business internally.

The Company prepares its financial statements using U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”) and investors should not directly compare with or infer a relationship from any of the Company’s operating results presented in accordance with GAAP to Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted diluted (loss) earnings per share and free cash flow. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations in their usefulness to investors because they have no standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and are not prepared under any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. In addition, other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of non-GAAP financial information as a tool for comparison. As a result, the non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only and should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information presented in accordance with GAAP.

About USANA
USANA develops and manufactures high-quality nutritional supplements, functional foods and personal care products that are sold directly to Brand Partners and Preferred Customers across 25 global markets. More information on USANA can be found at www.usana.com. USANA also owns a 78.8% controlling ownership stake in Hiya Health Products, a children's health and wellness company, and a 100% interest in Rise Wellness. Hiya and Rise Wellness offer a variety of clean-label health products. More information on Hiya can be found at www.hiyahealth.com. More information on Rise Wellness can be found at www.risebar.com and www.proteinpop.com.
