Vanda Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Q3 2026 Total Revenues increased 24% to $69.8 million compared to Q3 2025 and 38% compared to Q2 2026
•Q3 2026 Total Operating Expenses decreased by 18% to $93.3 million compared to Q2 2026
•Announced positive results from the Phase III study of NEREUS™ for prevention of GLP-1-induced vomiting
•NEREUS™ for prevention of vomiting induced by motion became commercially available in Q2 2026
•BYSANTITM commercial launch expected in Q4 2026
•QuimilzaTM BLA for GPP under review by the FDA; PDUFA target action date of December 12, 2026
WASHINGTON – October 28, 2026 – Vanda Pharmaceuticals Inc. (Vanda) (Nasdaq: VNDA) today announced financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We are pleased with the strong revenue growth in the third quarter, increasing 24% year-over-year, driven by continued Fanapt momentum and the early contribution from our NEREUS commercial launch, while operating expenses decreased 18% compared to the second quarter as key Phase III and launch-preparation activities conclude,” said Mihael H. Polymeropoulos, M.D., Vanda’s President, CEO and Chairman of the Board. “Looking ahead, with the commercial launch of BYSANTI expected in the fourth quarter, the Quimilza BLA under FDA review with a December PDUFA date, and positive Phase III results supporting potential expansions for NEREUS in GLP-1-induced vomiting and HETLIOZ in Delayed Sleep-Wake Phase Disorder, we are focused on further moderating costs and driving revenue growth from our current portfolio and planned launches to support sustainable long-term value.”
Financial Highlights
Operating Expenses
•During 2025 and 2026, Vanda has advanced multiple Phase III programs, continued its commercial activities for Fanapt® (iloperidone), HETLIOZ® (tasimelteon) and PONVORY® (ponesimod) and prepared for the commercial launches, including manufacturing commercial supplies, of NEREUSTM (tradipitant), BYSANTITM (milsaperidone), and QuimilzaTM (imsidolimab). These activities resulted in significantly increased operating expenses in 2025 and 2026. As certain of these activities began concluding in Q3 2026, operating expenses have decreased and Vanda expects operating expenses to decrease further in future quarters, as the remainder of these activities conclude.
Third Quarter of 2026
•Total net product sales were $69.8 million in Q3 2026, a 24% increase compared to $56.3 million in Q3 2025 and a 38% increase compared to $50.5 million in Q2 2026. Total net product sales includes approximately $7.0 million of HETLIOZ® revenue for orders shipped on June 29, 2026 that arrived on July 1, 2026.
•Total operating expenses were $93.3 million in Q3 2026, a 7% increase compared to $87.5 million in Q3 2025 and an 18% decrease compared to $114.3 million in Q2 2026.
•Fanapt® net product sales were $38.8 million in Q3 2026, a 24% increase compared to $31.2 million in Q3 2025 and an 8% increase compared to $36.0 million in Q2 2026.
•HETLIOZ® net product sales were $21.7 million in Q3 2026, a 21% increase compared to $18.0 million in Q3 2025 and a 289% increase compared to $5.6 million in Q2 2026, and includes orders totaling approximately $7.0 million in revenue that were shipped on June 29, 2026 and arrived on July 1, 2026. These orders were recognized as revenue in Q3 2026.


•PONVORY® net product sales were $7.7 million in Q3 2026, a 10% increase compared to $7.0 million in Q3 2025 and a 2% decrease compared to $7.9 million in Q2 2026.
•NEREUSTM net product sales were $1.6 million in Q3 2026, a 60% increase compared to $1.0 million in Q2 2026. Vanda launched NEREUSTM commercially in the U.S. in Q2 2026, with the direct-to-consumer offering via the web portal nereus.us, and began sales force promotion in Q3 2026.
•Loss before income taxes was $22.4 million in Q3 2026 compared with $28.4 million in Q3 2025.
•Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities (Cash) totaled $140.5 million as of September 30, 2026, representing a decrease to Cash of $29.5 million in Q3 2026.
First Nine Months of 2026
•Total net product sales were $172.1 million in the first nine months of 2026, an 8% increase compared to $158.9 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•Total operating expenses were $309.6 million in the first nine months of 2026, a 15% increase compared to $269.7 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•Fanapt® net product sales were $104.3 million in the first nine months of 2026, a 24% increase compared to $84.1 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•HETLIOZ® net product sales were $43.2 million in the first nine months of 2026, a 21% decrease compared to $55.0 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•PONVORY® net product sales were $21.9 million in the first nine months of 2026, an 11% increase compared to $19.8 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•NEREUSTM net product sales were $2.6 million in the first nine months of 2026.
•Loss before income taxes was $133.2 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared with $100.6 million in the first nine months of 2025.
•Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities (Cash) totaled $140.5 million as of September 30, 2026, representing a decrease to Cash of $123.4 million compared to December 31, 2025.
Key Commercial Highlights
•Fanapt® saw continued strong momentum in Q3 2026, with total prescriptions (TRx)1 up 25% and new-to-brand prescriptions (NBRx)1 up 18% versus Q3 2025. Since commercial expansion following the approval of bipolar disorder, Fanapt® has seen significant growth, with TRx1 up 72% and NBRx1 up 306% versus Q2 2024.
•BYSANTITM received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval for the treatment of bipolar I disorder and schizophrenia in Q1 2026 and is expected to launch in Q4 2026. BYSANTITM is protected by data exclusivity through February 20, 2031 and multiple patents, the latest of which expires on May 31, 2044.
•Vanda launched NEREUSTM commercially in the U.S. in Q2 2026, with the direct-to-consumer offering via the web portal nereus.us, and began sales force promotion in Q3 2026. During Q3 2026, the NEREUS™ launch showed strong growth in both total prescriptions and unique prescribers as compared to Q2 2026.
Key Regulatory & Clinical Development Highlights
Recent & Upcoming Milestones
•In October 2026, Vanda announced positive results from the Phase III clinical study, VP-VLY-686-3601, of NEREUS™ for the prevention of vomiting associated with GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy. Vanda intends to discuss the results with the FDA and plans to submit a supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) seeking approval of NEREUS™ for the prevention of vomiting associated with GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy.
•In September 2026, Vanda announced positive topline results from the pivotal Phase III study of HETLIOZ® in adults with Delayed Sleep-Wake Phase Disorder (DSWPD). Vanda intends to discuss these data with the FDA and to submit an sNDA seeking approval of HETLIOZ® for DSWPD. If approved, HETLIOZ® would be the first FDA-approved medicine indicated for this condition.


•The Biologics License Application (BLA) for Quimilza™ in Generalized Pustular Psoriasis (GPP) is under review by the FDA with a Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target action date of December 12, 2026. The results of the pivotal clinical study were published in the April 28, 2026 issue of the New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM) Evidence2.
•The Phase III study of BYSANTI™ as a once-daily adjunctive treatment for major depressive disorder (MDD) is ongoing, with results expected in the first half of 2027. The study is enrolling patients in 54 sites in the U.S. and Europe and has enrolled 55% of the 500 patient enrollment target. The primary endpoint of the study is the change from baseline to week 6 of the Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) comparing milsaperidone and placebo. Secondary endpoints include Clinical Global Impression – Severity (CGI-S) and Clinical Global Impression – Change (CGI-C).
Regulatory Proceedings
•Vanda continues to progress the FDA formal hearing regarding HETLIOZ® for the treatment of jet lag disorder. The proceeding, a rare administrative hearing process granted after the D.C. Circuit set aside the FDA’s prior refusal to approve the application, is advancing according to schedule and is expected to culminate in a five-day hearing before the Administrative Law Judge from December 14, 2026 to December 18, 2026.
•Vanda continues to pursue its legal challenge to FDA’s final order refusing to hold a hearing or to approve the Company’s sNDA for HETLIOZ® in the treatment of insomnia. Following oral argument before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit in September 2026, the matter is awaiting decision.
•Vanda continues to pursue its legal challenge to FDA’s final order refusing to hold a hearing or to approve the Company’s NDA for NEREUS™ in gastroparesis. Briefing is currently underway before the DC Circuit, and is due to be completed in mid-November.
GAAP Financial Results
Net loss was $22.6 million (diluted loss per share of $0.37) in Q3 2026 compared with a net loss of $22.6 million (diluted loss per share of $0.38) in Q3 2025 and a net loss of $62.5 million (diluted net loss per share of $1.04) in Q2 2026.
Net loss was $133.6 million (diluted loss per share of $2.23) in the first nine months of 2026 compared with a net loss of $79.3 million (diluted loss per share of $1.35) in the first nine months of 2025.
2026 Financial Guidance
Vanda is updating its 2026 financial guidance and expects to achieve total revenue for full year 2026 of greater than $240 million.
