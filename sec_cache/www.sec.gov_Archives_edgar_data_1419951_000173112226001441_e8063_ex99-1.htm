EXHIBIT 99.1

 

Data Storage Corporation Provides Third Quarter 2026 Business Update

 

Advances Development of AI Continuity Platform for Regulated Industries; Ends Quarter with
$9.5 Million in Cash and Marketable Securities and No Long-Term Debt

 

Conference Call to be Held Today at 11:00 a.m. ET

 

New York, N.Y., November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Data Storage Corporation (Nasdaq: DTST) (“DTST” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided a business update.

 

Following the September 2025 sale of its CloudFirst business, the Company has focused on leveraging its capital, technology expertise, and public company platform to pursue new growth opportunities, including the development of an AI continuity platform and the evaluation of strategic acquisitions and partnerships in cybersecurity and other complementary markets, intended to accelerate growth and build long-term shareholder value.

 

Recent Business Highlights

 

●Commenced Development of AI Continuity Control Platform: During the third quarter, the Company initiated development of its AI Continuity Control Platform, designed to help enterprises protect and recover AI workloads through automated failure detection, secure replication and on-demand GPU recovery, without requiring them to keep standby GPUs running at all times. The platform is being developed to address business continuity and regulatory resilience requirements, particularly in regulated industries such as healthcare and financial services.

 

●Strengthened Focus on Strategic Growth Opportunities: The Company continues to evaluate potential acquisitions, partnerships, and organic initiatives designed to establish new revenue streams, expand its technology capabilities, and leverage its existing infrastructure and industry experience.

 

●Maintained Financial Flexibility: As of September 30, 2026, the Company held approximately $9.5 million in cash and marketable securities with no long-term debt, providing resources to support ongoing operations and evaluate strategic growth initiatives. During the third quarter, the Company received the final $1.0 million of escrowed proceeds from the sale of the Company’s CloudFirst business.

 

●Nexxis Steady Performance: Third quarter revenue increased 4.3% compared to the second quarter of 2026. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenue increased 2.0% to $1.08 million and gross profit increased 6.8% to $0.51 million, with gross margin improving to 47.3% from 45.1% in the prior year period. Nexxis provides recurring voice, internet connectivity, and data transport services and serves as the Company’s operating foundation while management advances broader technology and growth initiatives.

 

Chuck Piluso, Chief Executive Officer of Data Storage Corporation, commented, “The third quarter marked continued progress in repositioning Data Storage Corporation for its next phase. Following the sale of our CloudFirst business and the completed tender offer, which returned approximately $29.3 million to shareholders, we have focused on putting our remaining capital, technology experience, and public company infrastructure to work in markets with significant long-term opportunity.”

 

 

 

“Our AI Continuity Control Platform builds on more than two decades of experience in compliance, business continuity and disaster recovery. As enterprises in regulated industries move AI into production, they will need to show they can quickly recover AI applications and systems when a process fails. We believe our platform addresses that need, and that cybersecurity is a natural complement to it. Together with Nexxis internet connectivity and AI voice solutions, we see these as the core of an integrated offering for regulated enterprises.”

 

“We ended the quarter with approximately $9.5 million in cash and marketable securities and no long-term debt. We remain focused on disciplined capital allocation, evaluating strategic opportunities, and building a business with the potential to deliver sustainable growth and long-term shareholder value. We look forward to updating shareholders as these initiatives progress.”

About Data Storage Corporation

 

Data Storage Corporation (Nasdaq: DTST), through its subsidiary Nexxis Inc., provides VoIP, internet access, SD-WAN, and data transport services as part of its integrated technology solutions platform. The Company is also pursuing strategic initiatives focused on AI continuity infrastructure for regulated industries, including the development of its AI Continuity Control Platform, which is intended to support recovery, resiliency, and compliance for sovereign AI and AI Factory environments.

 

DTST continues to evaluate strategic opportunities, including potential investments, partnerships, acquisitions, and other transactions focused on AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, telecommunications, and emerging enterprise technology markets. For more information, visit www.dtst.com.
