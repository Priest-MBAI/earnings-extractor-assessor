BRAEMAR HOTELS & RESORTS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

DALLAS, November 4, 2026 – Braemar Hotels & Resorts Inc. (NYSE: BHR) (“Braemar” or the “Company”) today reported financial results and performance measures for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The comparable performance measurements for Occupancy, Average Daily Rate (ADR), Revenue Per Available Room (RevPAR), and Hotel EBITDA assume each of the hotel properties in the Company’s hotel portfolio as of September 30, 2026 was owned as of the beginning of each of the periods presented. Unless otherwise stated, all reported results compare the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 with the third quarter ended September 30, 2025 (see discussion below). The reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures is included in the financial tables accompanying this press release.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
Q3 HOTEL FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
•RevPAR: Comparable RevPAR for all hotels totaled $284, an increase of 14.4% over the prior year quarter, which is the second-highest Q3 comparable RevPAR in the Company’s history, trailing only the post-pandemic recovery in 2022. Comparable ADR increased 8.6% over the prior year quarter to $419, and Comparable Occupancy increased 5.3% over the prior year quarter to 67.9%. Comparable RevPAR for all hotels excluding the Four Seasons Resort Scottsdale, which was sold in October 2026, totaled $290, an increase of 15.9% over the prior year quarter, with ADR of $411 and occupancy of 70.6%.
•Total RevPAR: Comparable Total RevPAR for all hotels increased 10.0% over the prior year quarter to $452, with comparable total hotel revenue up 9.9% to $95.2 million.
•Urban Properties: Comparable RevPAR increased 23.3% to $211, and Comparable Hotel EBITDA increased 63.2% to $10.1 million, with Comparable Hotel EBITDA margin expanding 706 basis points to 24.3%.
•Resort Properties: Comparable RevPAR increased 5.9% to $457, with Comparable ADR up 8.4% to $889.

•Year-to-Date: Comparable RevPAR for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased 10.0% to $366. Comparable Hotel EBITDA was $93.0 million, up 14.9%, with Comparable Hotel EBITDA margin expanding 158 basis points to 26.0%.
•Comparable Hotel EBITDA: $12.9 million for the quarter, up 20.7% over the prior year quarter. Comparable Hotel EBITDA margin expanded 122 basis points to 13.6%. Comparable Hotel EBITDA for all hotels excluding the Four Seasons Resort Scottsdale, which was sold in October 2026, totaled $14.0 million, an increase of 43.8% over the prior year quarter.
Q3 FINANCIAL RESULTS
•Net loss: Net loss attributable to common stockholders for the quarter was $240.9 million or ($3.51) per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $8.2 million or ($0.12) per diluted share in the prior year quarter, primarily due to the $480.0 million termination fee payable to our external advisor recorded during the quarter.
•Adjusted EBITDAre: $3.9 million for the quarter, compared with $16.4 million in the prior year quarter, which included hotels that have since been sold. Due to the portfolio’s seasonality, the third quarter is traditionally the Company’s lowest-earning quarter of the year.
•Adjusted funds from operations (AFFO): ($0.25) per diluted share for the quarter, compared with ($0.19) in the prior year quarter.
BALANCE SHEET & CAPITAL ALLOCATION
•Cash: The Company ended the quarter with cash and cash equivalents of $78.9 million and restricted cash of $39.6 million. The vast majority of the restricted cash consists of manager-held reserves. At the end of the quarter, there was also $16.5 million due from third-party hotel managers, which is primarily the Company’s cash held by one of its property managers and is also available to fund hotel operating costs.
•Leverage: Net debt to gross assets was 33.3% at the end of the third quarter. All of the Company’s $624.4 million of indebtedness is non-recourse, and the trailing twelve-month Comparable Hotel EBITDA debt yield was 19.0%.
•Capital investments: Capex invested during the quarter was $5.0 million.
•Preferred redemptions: During the quarter, the Company redeemed approximately $5.0 million of its non-traded preferred stock in cash. Redeemable preferred stock redemptions payable totaled $63.5 million as of September 30, 2026.
•Refinancing: On October 30, 2026, the Company refinanced its mortgage loan for the Ritz-Carlton Lake Tahoe, which had an existing outstanding balance of $43.4 million, a floating interest rate of SOFR + 3.25% and a final maturity date in January 2027. The new loan totals $60.0 million with an additional $15.0 million available to be drawn in the future subject to certain conditions, has a three-year initial term with two one-year extension options and provides for a floating interest rate of SOFR + 2.65%. With this financing now in place, the Company’s next hard debt maturity is not until December 2028.

RECENT OPERATING HIGHLIGHTS
•Following the recent conversion to Hilton’s LXR Hotels & Resorts collection, Cameo Beverly Hills delivered RevPAR growth of 82.8% over the prior year quarter.
•The Notary Hotel delivered RevPAR of $176, an increase of approximately 33.9% over the prior year quarter, with Hotel EBITDA of approximately $3.9 million, an increase of 72.5% over the prior year quarter.
•Sofitel Chicago Magnificent Mile delivered RevPAR of $276, an increase of approximately 18.0% over the prior year quarter, with Hotel EBITDA of approximately $4.7 million, an increase of 75.2% over the prior year quarter, and Hotel EBITDA margin expansion of 1,159 basis points to 34.9%.
•The Ritz-Carlton Reserve Dorado Beach delivered RevPAR of $1,025, an increase of approximately 14.4% over the prior year quarter.
•On July 14, 2026, the Company closed on the sale of the Ritz-Carlton Sarasota, the Hotel Yountville and the Bardessono Hotel and Spa for a total purchase price of $437.5 million in cash, subject to customary prorations and adjustments. In addition, on August 12, 2026, the Company closed on the sale of the Pier House Resort & Spa for $190.0 million ($1.3 million per key), subject to customary prorations and adjustments.
•Subsequent to quarter end, on October 28, 2026, the Company closed on the previously announced sale of the Four Seasons Resort Scottsdale at Troon North for $372 million ($1.8 million per key), subject to customary prorations and adjustments. The sale price represents a 5.8% capitalization rate on net operating income for the trailing 12 months ended September 2026, including anticipated capital expenditures of $23 million. This investment generated an unlevered return of approximately 15%, significantly above our 10% minimum target.

HOTEL EBITDA MARGINS AND QUARTERLY SEASONALITY TRENDS

The Company believes year-over-year Comparable Hotel EBITDA and Comparable Hotel EBITDA Margin comparisons are more meaningful to gauge the performance of the Company’s hotels than sequential quarter-over-quarter comparisons. To help investors better understand the substantial seasonality in the Company’s portfolio, the Company provides quarterly detail on its Comparable Hotel EBITDA and Comparable Hotel EBITDA Margin for the current and certain prior-year periods based upon the number of hotels in the Company’s portfolio as of the end of the current period. As the Company’s portfolio mix changes from time to time, so will the seasonality for Comparable Hotel EBITDA and Comparable Hotel EBITDA Margin.
