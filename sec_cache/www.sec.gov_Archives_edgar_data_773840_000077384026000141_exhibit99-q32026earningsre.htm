HONEYWELL TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS, RAISES FULL-YEAR 2026 OUTLOOK

•Sales of $5.0 billion, up 1% reported (7% organic1) driven by broad-based growth across all segments
•Operating margin of 14.8%, up 640 basis points; segment margin1 of 21.3%, up 370 basis points
•Earnings per share (EPS) of $2.40, down 20% due to one-time gain in prior year; adjusted EPS1 of $2.27, up 34%
•Operating cash of $0.6 billion; free cash flow1 of $0.7 billion, with conversion1 of 95%
•Raising full-year organic revenue, adjusted EPS and free cash flow guidance; expect segment margin to be at previous high end of guidance

CHARLOTTE, N.C., October 22, 2026 -- Honeywell Technologies (NASDAQ: HON) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
The company reported third-quarter sales of $5.0 billion, up 1% reported and 7% organic1, driven by broad-based strength across all three segments, pricing actions, and continued demand across key end markets. Backlog increased 7% year over year to nearly $19 billion, excluding Warehouse and Workflow Solutions (WWS) and Productivity Solutions and Services (PSS) divestitures in 3Q 2026.
Operating income increased 79% and segment profit1 increased 23% driven by productivity, including elimination of stranded costs related to prior spin-offs and divestitures, pricing actions that more than offset inflation, and volume leverage. Operating margin of 14.8% increased 640 basis points and segment margin1 of 21.3% expanded 370 basis points, led by Industrial Automation and Building Automation.
Reported EPS decreased 20% primarily due to a one-time gain from the Resideo termination agreement in prior year. Excluding the impact of this and other items, adjusted earnings per share1 increased 34% year over year to $2.27 driven by organic sales growth, higher segment profit, and lower net interest expense.
Finally, operating cash flow of $607 million decreased 74% primarily due to the one-time settlement from the Resideo termination agreement in prior year, while free cash flow1 of $688 million increased 15%, with free cash flow conversion1of 95%, driven by higher operating income and lower capital expenditures.

Management Commentary
“Honeywell Technologies delivered strong results across the business in our first quarter as a standalone company. Continued momentum across all three segments, together with our improving position in high-growth verticals, translated to 7% organic sales growth, above the three-year target set at our June Investor Day. This performance, coupled with continued progress reducing fixed cost, enabled adjusted operating income to increase at more than three times the rate of sales, while adjusted EPS rose 34%, well ahead of our expectations for the quarter. As a result of this strong performance, we are raising our full-year 2026 outlook, which gives me great confidence in our long-term strategy and our ability to deliver on our 2029 targets,” said Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Vimal Kapur.

Building Automation sales grew 8% organically1 year over year. Building products grew 11%, fueled by the fire and security businesses, while building solutions grew 4% on continued strength in services. Segment margin expanded 30 basis points to 27.0%, benefiting from volume leverage and pricing actions that offset inflation.
Process Automation and Technology sales increased 8% organically1 year over year. Project sales grew 14% led by continued growth in LNG and petrochemical projects, while aftermarket grew 4% driven primarily by refining demand. Margins contracted 210 basis points as expected driven by project mix and dilution from the acquisition of Johnson Matthey’s Catalyst Technologies business.
Industrial Automation sales grew 4% organically1 year over year led by 16% growth in solutions, driven primarily by industrial measurement and utilities. Products were flat year over year with double-digit growth in sensing largely offset by industry-wide meter deployment deferrals in utilities. Segment margin expanded 350 basis points year over year to 18.4%, driven primarily by price and productivity actions, partially offset by inflation.

Revised 2026 Guidance
Honeywell Technologies is raising its full-year outlook for organic revenue, adjusted EPS and free cash flow, and also now expects segment margin to be at the previous high end of guidance, following strong third quarter performance and continued momentum across the portfolio. The company now expects full-year sales of $20.0 billion to $20.1 billion with organic1 sales growth of 4% to 5%, and 5% to 6% organic1 growth in the fourth quarter; segment margin1 of approximately 20.5%, at the upper end of its previous guidance range, representing segment margin1 expansion of approximately 290 basis points; and adjusted earnings per share1 of $8.40 to $8.45, up 30% to 31%, representing a $0.23 increase from the midpoint of its previous guidance range. Finally, operating cash flow and free cash flow are now expected to be ~$2.1 billion each.

About Honeywell Technologies
Honeywell Technologies is a global, pure-play automation company with a legacy of innovating to help solve the world's most mission-critical challenges, enhancing the quality of life for people and communities around the world. We serve the building, industrial, and process sectors with a broad portfolio of services, solutions, and products, underpinned by our Honeywell Technologies Accelerator operating system and Honeywell Technologies Forge intelligence layer. By combining the deep domain expertise of our more than 50,000 employees with decades of data from our global installed base, we are uniquely positioned to lead the industrial sector's transition from automation to autonomy. For more news and information on Honeywell Technologies, please visit Honeywell Technologies Newsroom.
