Kyntra Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

•
Completed restructuring of royalty financing agreement, reducing the maximum aggregate payment obligation by $60 million, with only $15 million in future royalty-backed payments remaining
•
Initiation of the pivotal Phase 3 trial of roxadustat for the treatment of anemia in LR-MDS patients with high transfusion burden (HTB) planned in 1Q 2027, subject to the execution of either a strategic partnership or an external capital raise
•
Decision to discontinue development of FG-3246 and FG-3180 following insufficient efficacy at the pre-specified Phase 2 interim analysis in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), resulting in a reduction of over $20 million in operating expenses in 2027 and 2028
•
Cash, cash equivalents, investments, and accounts receivable of $44.1 million as of September 30, 2026, with a cash runway now into 2028
•
Kyntra Bio to host conference call and webcast presentation today at 5:00 PM ET

 

SAN FRANCISCO, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Kyntra Bio (Nasdaq: KYNB) today reported financial results for the third quarter 2026 and provided an update on the company’s recent developments.

“Following a pre-planned interim analysis showing insufficient efficacy, we have made the decision to terminate the FG-3246 Phase 2 trial,” said Thane Wettig, Chief Executive Officer of Kyntra Bio. “We continue to work towards the completion of our transformation to a pure-play, rare disease company laser-focused on the initiation and execution of the Phase 3 roxadustat trial. The unmet medical need and corresponding multi-billion-dollar commercial opportunity in LR-MDS is significant. Subject to the execution of either a strategic partnership or an external capital raise, we plan on initiating the pivotal Phase 3 trial in the first quarter of 2027.”

 

Key Highlights of Third Quarter, Recent Developments, and Upcoming Milestones

Roxadustat

•
Initiation of the pivotal Phase 3 trial of roxadustat for the treatment of anemia in patients with LR-MDS and HTB planned in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the execution of either a strategic partnership or an external capital raise.

 

FG-3246 (CD46 Targeting ADC) and FG-3180 (CD46 Targeting PET Imaging Agent)

•
Following a pre-specified interim analysis of the ongoing Phase 2 study of FG-3246 in mCRPC, the Company decided to close enrollment in the study and discontinue development of the program.
o
Preliminary efficacy results at the interim analysis did not meet the threshold required to support continued development.
•
The Company plans to complete the close-out of the study over the coming months and will cancel all planned future investment in the program.
•
The Company expects to save over $20 million in operating expenses in 2027 and 2028 from the termination of the study.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Corporate

•
In August 2026, the company signed an amendment and restatement of its existing royalty financing agreement with NQ Project Phoebus, L.P., materially reducing its payment obligations under the agreement in exchange for an accelerated upfront payment. The amendment included the following terms:
o
Reduction of the maximum aggregate payments under the agreement from $125 million to $65 million, including the $42.6 million accelerated payment to NQ Project Phoebus, L.P., bringing total payments made to date to $50 million, a full return of NQ Project Phoebus, L.P.’s invested capital.
o
Remaining payments, capped at $15 million, to be paid from 50% of the roxadustat drug product revenue Kyntra Bio recognizes from Astellas in the Astellas territories excluding Japan.
o
Once the $15 million cap is reached, the amended agreement will terminate, with Kyntra Bio retaining all subsequent EVRENZO™ royalties in the Astellas territories.
•
Appointed Andrew E. Singer, MBA to the Board of Directors.

 

 

Financial

•
Total revenue from continuing operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.8 million, as compared to $1.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
Net income from continuing operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.2 million, or $0.04 net income per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $13.1 million, or $3.25 net loss per basic and diluted share, one year ago.
•
As of September 30, 2026, Kyntra Bio reported $44.1 million in cash, cash equivalents, investments, and accounts receivable.
•
The Company expects its cash, cash equivalents, investments, and accounts receivable to be sufficient to fund operating plans into 2028.

About Roxadustat

Roxadustat, an oral medication, is the first in a new class of medicines comprising HIF-PH inhibitors that promote erythropoiesis, or red blood cell production, through increased endogenous production of erythropoietin, improved iron absorption and mobilization, and downregulation of hepcidin.

 

Roxadustat is approved in Europe, Japan, China, and numerous other countries for the treatment of anemia of CKD in adult patients on dialysis (DD) and not on dialysis (NDD). Kyntra Bio has the sole rights to roxadustat in the United States, Canada, Mexico, and in all markets not held by AstraZeneca or licensed to Astellas. Astellas and Kyntra Bio are collaborating on the commercialization of roxadustat for the treatment of anemia in territories including Japan, Europe, Turkey, Russia, and the Commonwealth of Independent States, the Middle East, and South Africa.

 

Following promising results from the Phase 2/3 MATTERHORN study, the Company is planning to study roxadustat in a pivotal Phase 3 trial in patients with anemia associated with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (LR-MDS) and high transfusion burden (HTB).

 

About Kyntra Bio

Kyntra Bio is a pure play, rare disease biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of roxadustat for anemia associated with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (LR-MDS). Roxadustat (爱瑞卓®, EVRENZO™) is currently approved in Europe, Japan, China, and numerous other countries for the treatment of anemia in chronic kidney disease (CKD) patients on dialysis and not on dialysis. The Company is planning to study roxadustat in a pivotal Phase 3 trial in patients with anemia associated with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (LR-MDS). For more information, please visit www.kyntrabio.com.
